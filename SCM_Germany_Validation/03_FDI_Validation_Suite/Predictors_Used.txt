Predictors used in Strict Max-Statistic FDI Validation Suite:
-------------------------------------------------------------
Data Range: 1980-2022
Outcome Variables: fdi_outflows_pct, fdi_inflows_pct (raw % of GDP from World Bank, plus asinh transformed variants)
Donor Pool: 11 non-European OECD countries (strictly excluding Mexico due to missing values during validation period)

Covariate Predictors:
1. Avg_GDPsz: Mean GDP (current USD) over pre-treatment window [1980:(t-1)]
2. Avg_GDPpc: Mean GDP per capita (current USD) over pre-treatment window [1980:(t-1)]
3. Avg_Pop: Mean population over pre-treatment window [1980:(t-1)]
4. GDPpc_Gr: Average GDPpc growth rate over pre-treatment window [1980:(t-1)]

Exact Outcome and Economic Lags (Strictly Before t):
5. L5_FDI, L5_GDPpc (at t-5)
6. L4_FDI, L4_GDPpc (at t-4)
7. L3_FDI, L3_GDPpc (at t-3)
8. L2_FDI, L2_GDPpc (at t-2)
9. L1_FDI, L1_GDPpc (at t-1)

Important Notes:
- To avoid restricting the solver too much, the long-term average of the outcome variable (Avg_FDI) is NOT used. Only exact 5-year lags are used to match the pre-intervention trajectory.
- ZERO look-ahead data leakage guaranteed! No interpolation/extrapolation across the treatment onset.
